How this calculator works

This tool projects your retirement savings by applying your estimated annual return monthly and adding your monthly contribution, starting from your current savings balance. It then estimates a sustainable first-year withdrawal using the "4% rule" — a commonly referenced (but not guaranteed) starting framework based on historical U.S. market data.

For more context on how this framework works and its limitations, read our guide on how much you actually need to retire.
